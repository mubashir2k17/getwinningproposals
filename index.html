03 / Process

What happens after you reach out.

You review the deliverables, set the final price, and submit the bid.

  1. Talk through the bid

    Tell us where the job is, when it’s due, and which parts of the estimate you’d like help with.

  2. Share drawings privately

    After the call, we’ll agree on a private way to share the drawings, specifications, and addenda.

  3. Agree on the quote

    We review the files and confirm the fee, scope, delivery date, and revision terms in writing before starting.

  4. Review the handoff

    Check the marked-up plans, workbook, or proposal draft. We flag open questions and assumptions for your review.

04 / FAQ

Before you send a bid our way.

How much does a project cost?

We quote each job after reviewing the drawing set, deliverables, complexity, and deadline. Takeoff-only and proposal-only work can be quoted separately.

How fast can you turn it around?

Share your bid deadline and time zone in the initial inquiry. We’ll confirm whether the job fits and agree on a delivery date after reviewing the drawings.

What happens if an addendum changes the scope?

Send the addendum as soon as it arrives. We’ll confirm what’s covered by the agreed revision terms and discuss any fee or schedule change before doing extra work.

Whose labor rates and material prices do you use?

We agree on the inputs with you and identify supplier pricing, dates, and assumptions in the workbook. You review those inputs and approve the final bid price.

How should I share confidential drawings?

Keep your first inquiry to basic project details. After the call, we’ll agree on how to transfer the drawings privately and discuss any access or handling requirements.
